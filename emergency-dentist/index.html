What counts as a dental emergency?

If something hurts, has broken or looks swollen, call us. These are the problems we see most often.

Severe toothacheOngoing or throbbing pain can mean decay, infection or an irritated nerve.
Cracked or broken toothCracks can spread if left. Many broken teeth can be repaired with a same day crown.
Knocked-out toothTime matters. The sooner we see you, the better the chance of saving the tooth.
Swelling or abscessSwelling, a bad taste or fever can be signs of infection, which needs treating quickly.
Lost filling or crownAn exposed tooth can be sensitive and break more easily. Keep the crown if you have it.
Wisdom tooth painPain or swelling at the back of the mouth is often a wisdom tooth coming through.

What to do before you get here

Call us first, then follow these steps on the way in.

Knocked-out adult tooth
  • Pick it up by the crown, not the root
  • If it's dirty, rinse it briefly in milk
  • Gently push it back into place if you can, or keep it in milk
  • Come straight in
Don't put a baby tooth back in. Bring your child in to be checked.
Broken or chipped tooth
  • Rinse your mouth with warm water
  • Keep any pieces and bring them with you
  • Use a cold pack on your cheek for swelling
  • Avoid chewing on that side
Toothache or swelling
  • Rinse with warm salty water
  • Use a cold pack on the outside of your face
  • Don't put heat on a swollen area
  • Call us as early in the day as you can
Go to hospital or call 000 if swelling is affecting your breathing or swallowing, bleeding won't stop, or you have a serious facial injury.

Emergency questions

Can you see me today?

We keep emergency appointments free every weekday. Call 07 5563 3133 as early as you can, as those spots fill through the day.

How much does an emergency appointment cost?

It depends on what you need. Your dentist explains the costs before any treatment starts, your health fund is claimed on the spot with HICAPS, and Zip and Afterpay Plus are available.

Do I need to be an existing patient?

No. New patients are welcome to call for an emergency appointment.

Are you open on weekends?

We're open Monday to Friday. For severe swelling, bleeding that won't stop or a facial injury outside our hours, go to your nearest hospital emergency department or call 000.
