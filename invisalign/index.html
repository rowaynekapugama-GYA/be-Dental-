Is Invisalign right for you?

Clear aligners suit many teens and adults with:

  • Crowded or crooked teeth
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Teeth that have shifted since braces
  • Some bite issues

If your case needs more complex care, we will say so at your consult and refer you to a trusted local orthodontist.

Invisalign questions

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Your Invisalign consultation is free, and there is no obligation to go ahead.

How long will it take?

From around six months for small adjustments to 18 to 24 months for more complex bite changes. Wearing your aligners as directed is the biggest factor.

Is Invisalign suitable for teenagers?

Often, yes. We assess every teen individually and talk parents through options, compliance and costs.

Can I see my result before I commit?

Your 3D scan produces a simulation of how your teeth are planned to move, so you can see the expected direction of treatment before you decide. Individual results vary.
