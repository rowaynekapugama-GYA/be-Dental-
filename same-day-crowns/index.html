When a crown is the right fix

Once more than half of a tooth has been lost to decay, fractures or fillings, a crown protects what is left. Common reasons include:

  • A tooth that has broken or cracked
  • A large old filling that is failing
  • Protecting a tooth after root canal treatment
  • Teeth worn down by grinding
  • Improving the shape or colour of a tooth

Same day crown questions

How much does a same day crown cost?

A CEREC crown at Be Dental is from $1,850. You get an exact quote before we start, your health fund rebate is claimed on the spot, and you can spread the cost with Zip or Afterpay Plus.

Does it hurt?

The tooth is numbed before we begin, so most people feel pressure rather than pain. Because there is no temporary crown, there is no second round of numbing either.

How long will my crown last?

Crowns often last 10 years or more with good home care and regular check-ups.
