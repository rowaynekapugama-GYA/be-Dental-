What happens at your first visit

  1. 1Fill in your forms before you arrive. Your medical history form is online, so you can skip the clipboard.
  2. 2A thorough check-up and digital x-rays. Your dentist looks at teeth, gums and bite, and talks you through what they see.
  3. 3A professional scale and clean. Plaque and tartar removed, teeth polished, home care tips that suit you.
  4. 4A clear plan, with prices, before anything else. If you need more treatment, you get a written quote first and no pressure.

Questions new patients ask

Do you take my health fund?

Yes. We are a preferred provider for Medibank, HCF and Smile.com.au, and accept every other fund through HICAPS so you only pay the gap on the day.

Can I bring the whole family?

Of course. Tell us when you book and we will line up appointments back to back where we can. Eligible children can be bulk billed under the Child Dental Benefits Schedule.

I'm nervous about the dentist. What can you do?

Let us know when you book. We take things at your pace, explain each step before we start, and you can stop at any time.

What if I have a dental emergency?

Call 07 5563 3133 as early as you can. We keep time aside for same day emergency appointments, and can often repair a broken tooth with a same day crown.
