Implant questions

Does getting an implant hurt?

The area is fully numbed, and many patients find placement more comfortable than having a tooth taken out. We talk you through each step and mild oral sedation is available if you are anxious.

Am I suitable for implants?

It depends on your bone, gum health, medical history and whether you smoke. Your 3D scan shows us how much bone there is, and if you need a graft we can usually do it here.

How long does treatment take?

Usually 3 to 6 months from consultation to finished crown, depending on healing and whether grafting is needed.

How long do implants last?

With good home care and regular check-ups, implants can last for decades.
